# Voice and likeness consent record (template)

This template is a starting point for C06·M01 and C16·M02. It is not legal advice. Laws differ by country and
state, and union agreements (for example SAG-AFTRA) can add rules. Have a lawyer check it before client work.

Store the signed record in `rights/consent/` and add a line to the rights log:
`python starter/tools/promptlog.py rights --id CONSENT-<NAME>-<DATE> --source "<person>" --license "consent record" --use "<scope>" --consent-file rights/consent/<file>.pdf`
