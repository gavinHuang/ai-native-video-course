## About this course

The capstone shows everything you learned in one project. You choose one of three briefs (or write your own at the same level). You plan, produce, deliver, and document. The rubric is published, so you can assess yourself before you finish.

**Starter tools for this course** (in the [starter kit](starter.zip); tested on 2026-10-04):

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| `templates/capstone/risk_register.md` | Risk register with early signs (M01) |
| `templates/capstone/delivery.json`, `rubric_scores.json` | The delivery manifest and the rubric score sheet (M02) |
| `tools/capstone_check.py` | Completeness check of the delivery package: plan, specs, C2PA, captions, stems, logs, write-up, rubric (M02) |
| All earlier tools | `qc_gate.py`, `spec_check.py`, `rights_audit.py`, `provenance.py`, `cost_model.py`, `model_trial.py` |
