# Research: Similar Courses

Date: 2026-10-04
Purpose: find existing courses and take the best parts for our self-paced course.

## 1. Pearson BTEC International Level 2 — Unit 4: Digital Video Production

- Source: https://qualifications.pearson.com/content/dam/pdf/btec-international-level-2/creative-media-production/2022/teaching-learning-materials/unit-4-sow.pdf
- Size: 60 guided learning hours, 30 lessons of 2 hours.
- Structure:
  - Learning aim A: purposes, features, and platforms of digital video.
    - A1: purposes (entertain, inform/educate, promote/persuade), types (TV, film, tutorial, ad, music video), genres, genre codes (mise-en-scène, camera, style, audio).
    - A2: digital video platforms.
  - Learning aim B: plan and shoot footage (B1 planning/pre-production, B2 shooting footage).
  - Learning aim C: edit and publish (C1 editing, C2 exporting and publishing).
- **What we take:**
  - The main sequence: purpose → plan → shoot → edit → publish. (Modules 1, 3–5, 9–11.)
  - Exercise: "Shots that make meaning". Groups shoot 5-second clips to show a feeling (for example, an extreme close-up of fingers to show nervousness). (Module 5.)
  - A learning log: the student records decisions during the work. (Every practice task.)

## 2. Pearson BTEC Level 3 National — Creative Digital Media Production

- Source: https://qualifications.pearson.com/en/qualifications/btec-nationals/creative-digital-media-production-2016.html
- Mandatory units: Media Representations, Pre-Production Portfolio, Responding to a Commission.
- **What we take:** Unit 8 "Responding to a Commission" — how to read a client brief, make ideas, and answer the client. (Module 14.) Pre-production portfolio idea. (Module 3.)

## 3. Sundance Institute — Mobile Filmmaking (free, self-paced)

- Source: https://collab.sundance.org/catalog/Mobile-Filmmaking-Presented-by-Xiaomi-On-Demand
- 7 sessions: introduction; pre-production; shooting techniques; gear; production on location; post-production; finishing and release.
- **What we take:** phone settings and phone limits (Module 4); low-cost and DIY gear (Modules 4, 6, 7); shooting in difficult conditions (Module 5).

## 4. Graduate School USA — Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced)

- Source: https://www.graduateschool.edu/outlines/video-production-course-online-self-paced-syllabus.pdf
- 14 modules: story and releases; casting; location; budget; phone as pro camera; composition and coverage; lighting; audio and AI cleanup; interviews; workflow and delivery; crew roles; live events, real estate, music videos; digital platforms; AI and responsible use.
- **What we take:** release forms (Module 13); audio cleanup with AI tools (Module 7); interviews (Module 8); responsible AI use (Module 13); example job types for the client module (Module 14).
- **What we skip:** casting, budgets, crew roles (not needed for a solo creator at beginner level).

## 10. Notes

- Common sequence in all courses: idea → plan → shoot (camera, light, sound) → edit → publish. Our module list follows it.
- Self-paced courses (Sundance, Graduate School) use short video + activity per session. Our module structure does the same.
- Vocational courses (BTEC) add: purpose/audience analysis, client briefs, and a portfolio. These help students get work.
