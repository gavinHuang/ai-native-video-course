# Studio policy for AI-generated media (template, C16·M04)

Adapt it, sign it as a team, and review it once a year. It is not legal advice.

## 1. We never make
- Sexual or intimate content of real people without their documented consent.
- Realistic fake statements, actions, or events involving real people, presented as real.
- Content that targets, harasses, or humiliates a private individual.
- The likeness of a child without guardian consent and a written safeguard plan.
- Realistic fake news, disasters, or emergencies presented as real.
- Content that copies a recognizable protected work, character, or trademark without a license.

## 2. Needs written consent or a license first
- Any real person's face, body, or voice (C16·M02).
- Client assets in third-party AI services (the project data rule, C13·M05).
- Music, stock, fonts, and model files with restricted licenses (C16·M01).

## 3. Disclosure
- We follow EU AI Act Article 50 and the rules of each platform and market.
- Realistic synthetic people or events get a visible label; every delivery is signed with C2PA content credentials (C16·M03).
- We never remove a provider's watermark.

## 4. Review of people and stories
- Casting decisions (age, gender, skin tone, body, accent) are written in the bible, not left to model defaults.
- Every 50 generated people, we review the pattern: who appears in which roles, and who is missing.

## 5. Footprint
- We explore at low cost (small sizes, few steps) and generate finals only for chosen takes.
- We report GPU energy per finished minute for our own compute and API seconds for hosted models.

## 6. Refusing and changing requests
- Anyone on the team can stop a job that may break this policy. The producer decides within 1 working day.
- We offer a changed design where possible (an illustrated style, a clear label, real data), and refuse in writing where not.

## 7. Incidents
- If our content is misused (re-posted without its label, cut to mislead), we record it, report it to the platform, and tell the client.
