## About this course

A model makes exactly what you describe, and invents the rest. A director who knows screen language describes more, so the model invents less. This course gives you the vocabulary of film and the reasons behind it. You will use this vocabulary in every prompt, every shot list, and every review.

There is no camera work in this course. You analyse existing films, and you make the shots with image and video models.
